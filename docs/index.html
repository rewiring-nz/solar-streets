Where this data comes from

A quick summary, not the full technical detail — see ARCHITECTURE.md on GitHub for that.

Solar installs

  • Real counts from EMI (the Electricity Authority), published monthly by street.
  • Street positions are matched to real OpenStreetMap roads — about 97% match; the rest are mostly private lanes, rural roads, or recent renames.
  • Region % is a real EMI figure (they publish both solar count and total connections at that level).
  • Town % is an estimate, always marked "est" — EMI doesn't publish a real total that fine, so it's modelled from real Census dwelling growth instead.
  • Battery counts are real EMI figures, but only published at region level — town popups show their region's rate, not a town-specific count.

Electric vehicles

  • From NZTA's live vehicle register — a real join (electric count vs total local fleet), not modelled, down to district level.

Keeping it current

  • Refreshes automatically every week. EMI and NZTA publish monthly, so it's never more than a few weeks stale.
